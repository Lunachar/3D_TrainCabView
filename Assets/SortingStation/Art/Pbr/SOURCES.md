# PBR materials

Textures in `Assets/SortingStation/Resources/Pbr` come from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com)
and are licensed **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** — no attribution required.

Downloaded 2026-09-25 at 1K resolution and repacked for URP Lit:

- `<Id>_Color.jpg` — base colour (sRGB).
- `<Id>_Normal.jpg` — OpenGL normal map (`NormalGL`).
- `<Id>_Mask.png` — linear: R = metalness, G = ambient occlusion, A = smoothness (1 − roughness).
  The same texture is assigned to `_MetallicGlossMap` and `_OcclusionMap`.
