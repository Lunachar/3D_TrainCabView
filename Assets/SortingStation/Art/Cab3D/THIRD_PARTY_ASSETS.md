# Cab 3D asset register

The first prototype uses only geometry and materials generated inside this project. No third-party model is included yet.

Before adding a free external asset, record:

| Asset | Author | Source URL | License | Changes | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| _Example_ | _Name_ | _URL_ | _CC0 / CC-BY / other_ | _LOD, recolour, atlas_ | _Forest, station, tunnel_ |

Assets with unclear or non-commercial licenses must not be committed to the game.
